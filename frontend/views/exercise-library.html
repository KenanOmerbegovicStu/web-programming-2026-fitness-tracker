Bench Press

Strength Chest

Press a barbell from the chest while lying on a flat bench.

How to perform

  1. Lie on the bench with your eyes under the bar and feet flat on the floor.
  2. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width and unrack it.
  3. Lower the bar to the middle of your chest with control.
  4. Press it back up until your arms are straight.
